### 5. Lightcone Chi Allocator — foundation complete

Design: binary white/black allocation
- Inside lightcone (d(v,center) <= v_hop * n_steps): chi_max
- Outside lightcone: chi=1 (exact, identity tensor)
- Single frontier shared across all states (required for inner products)

New methods in include/grid.hpp:
  Grid::bfs(center)              — BFS distances from center
  Grid::apply_lightcone(...)     — sets bond.chi = chi_max or 1
  Grid::lightcone_memory_mb(...) — memory estimate before allocation
  Grid::in_lightcone(...)        — static helper

Test: test/test_lightcone_alloc.cpp — 18/18 PASS

Memory savings measured:
  Heavy-hex chi=70, 10 cycles: 94.86 MB -> 23.13 MB (4x, v=0.376 noiseless)
  Rect 7x7  chi=50, 10 cycles: 2541 MB  -> 100 MB   (25x, v=0.097 noisy N3)

Backward compatible: center=-1 or v_hop<=0 -> full allocation (current behavior).
