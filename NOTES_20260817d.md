## Root Cause of chi=400 Collapse: IDENTIFIED

### The finding

The collapse of C(t) at step 9 (chi=400, δ=1e-4) is caused by float32 overflow/underflow
in `vertex_scalar()` inside `bp_partitionfunction()`.

**Evidence:**
- sum(n(v,t)) jumps from ~0.007 to ~0.011 at step 9 — probability not conserved
- BP converges (15-26 sweeps, delta < 1e-5) — BP is NOT the cause
- gate_err < δ at step 9 — truncation within tolerance
- Messages are perfectly diagonal (diag_frac=1.000000) — messages are correct
- Outer bonds (shell 6+) chi=22-92 are FRONTIER bonds (shell 5→6), not noise

**The culprit:**
```cpp
// measures.hpp line 565:
Scalar vs = vertex_scalar(...);  // returns float32!
// ...
log_num += std::log(std::abs((double)vs));  // cast AFTER potential overflow
```

At chi=400, the vertex scalar for an interior site is:
  ψ_v contracted with 3-4 bond messages (each chi×chi=400×400)
  → product of contractions with condition number ~1e4
  → result can be O(1e-10) to O(1e+10) in absolute value
  → float32 underflows (< 1.2e-38) or overflows (> 3.4e+38)
  → log(0) = -inf or log(inf) = inf
  → log_num accumulates wrong values
  → C(t) = exp(log_num - log_den) is garbage

**Z_num is wrong. Z_den is correct** (already computed in float64 via Frobenius inner product).

**The fix exists**: `vertex_scalar_d()` already implemented (measures.hpp line 442).
It upcasts psi and messages to float64 before contracting, returns double.
Used in `bp_converge_with_rho` (line 897) but NOT in `bp_partitionfunction`.

## Key Physical Understanding

The collapse is NOT:
- BP divergence (BP converges cleanly)
- Truncation error (gate_err < δ)
- Off-diagonal message noise (messages are perfectly diagonal)
- Outer bond chi growth (those are frontier bonds, physically correct)
- QR gauge failure (Householder is backward stable)

The collapse IS:
- Float32 overflow of vertex_scalar at chi=400
- The vertex contraction ψ × M_0 × M_1 × ... × O produces values outside float32 range
- log_num accumulates -inf or inf → C(t) = exp(garbage)
