## Key Results

### 1. measure_vhop.py: accurate frontier velocity

The linear-fit v_hop (slope) systematically underestimates the true frontier:
  Heavy-hex 10 cycles: slope=0.376, actual max_shell=5, v_hop_eff=0.500
  Heavy-hex 20 cycles: slope=0.221, actual max_shell=6, v_hop_eff=0.300

Use measure_vhop.py to extract the correct value from a pre-flight run.
Rule: pre-flight chi must not saturate (chi=50 sufficient for heavy-hex 20 cycles).
Rule: measure v_hop for the same n_steps as the target simulation.

### 2. --v_hops=a,b,c,d: per-state frontier

Noisy states have slower frontiers than noiseless:
  NL: shell 5, N1-N3: shell 3 at 10 cycles
  NL: shell 6, N1-N3: shell 3-4 at 20 cycles

Per-state allocation saves more memory/compute but is NOT safe for MPF:
  BP coupling propagates truncation errors from outside to inside the lightcone
  Inside error ~7e-05 when per-state; ~2e-06 for shared frontier
  MPF entropy loss inflated by ~20% with per-state allocation

### 3. Shared frontier: strictly better than full allocation

The shared frontier (noiseless v_hop, covers all states) is not just safe — it is
physically more correct than full allocation:

  C(t): removes accumulated numerical leakage from outside-lightcone sites
         (7.8e-05 max at 20 cycles, grows with cycle count)
  Inner products: enforces exact causal structure, biases MPF less
  Memory: 35% -> 23% VRAM at chi=200 (~3.8 GB saved)
  Compute: 4-7% speedup (skipped gates)

### 4. Why leakage outside the lightcone in full allocation

Physical wave is exactly zero outside Trotter frontier (hard boundary = n_steps hops).
BUT: BP messages are initialized and updated for ALL bonds in full allocation.
BLAS operations accumulate O(1e-7) roundoff per operation, propagated through
message passing network for 20+ cycles → O(1e-5 to 1e-4) accumulated leakage.

Lightcone allocation eliminates this: chi_alloc=1 bonds skip all operations →
no roundoff accumulation → exactly zero outside lightcone.

### 5. Chi saturation at 20 cycles

chi=200 saturates at step 12 for heavy-hex. Steps 12-20 are at full chi=200.
Per-step cost at saturation: ~2-3.5s (variable due to GPU scheduling noise).
Total: Full=37.0s, Shared=35.1s (5% saving at chi=200, 20 cycles).

The smaller speedup vs 10 cycles (5% vs 7%) is because BP sweeps dominate at
saturation and BP does NOT yet skip outside-lightcone bonds — next target.

### 6. Information-starved regime persists at 20 cycles

Heavy-hex with hardware noise remains information-starved even at 20 cycles.
MPF coefficients still [-1,1,1], entropy MPF = frontier MPF, 0% improvement.
Only 21/68 sites inside lightcone — increasing cycles helps slightly (shell 6 vs 5)
but the sparse graph means most of the lattice is never reached.
